# Product brief

## Product
AccaForge is an evidence-led football analysis and match-market information platform. It publishes four independently validated daily products:

- **10-Leg Accumulator:** ten different fixtures; each leg priced 1.20–1.50; all ten active legs must win.
- **Super 5:** five different fixtures; each leg priced 1.50–2.00.
- **Super 10:** ten different fixtures; each leg priced 1.50–2.00.
- **All-Matches Challenge:** one defensible priced selection for every eligible fixture on the daily slate. A single PASS, missing decision or invalid selection withholds the entire edition.

Combined odds are the mathematical outcome of the selected legs, not a generation target. The AI must choose among supplied markets based on evidence; it must never select a market merely because it exists or has the highest raw probability.

## Audience
Adults aged 18+ who follow domestic and international football, initially with a mobile-first experience and Kenya-friendly pricing/payment options.

## Jobs to be done
- Understand the day's published football analysis and market options.
- Inspect fixture, market, selection, bookmaker price, estimated probability and rationale.
- Compare the four distinct product formats without confusing their risk profiles.
- Review settled selections and accumulator-level track record.
- Save fixtures and receive useful match/market updates (Premium roadmap).
- Understand that prices move and football outcomes are uncertain.

## Product character
Precise, composed, analytical, transparent and football-literate. Avoid hype, fabricated certainty and "sure bet" language.

## Product constraints
- Football only.
- Adults 18+ only, with responsible-play messaging.
- No promises, guarantees or claims of certain returns.
- Never expose private data providers, model names, keys or internal processing details in public UI.
- Results must come from stored, validated data. The browser never invents or recalculates settlement outcomes.
- Premium benefits must be real, measurable research or convenience features—not inflated win-rate claims or access to supposedly guaranteed picks.
- Paid entitlements must be checked server-side; hiding UI elements is not access control.
