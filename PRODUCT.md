# Product brief

## Product
AccaForge publishes three daily football accumulators, with decimal combined-odds targets of 10.00, 15.00 and 20.00, for adults who follow football and want a clear, structured view of upcoming markets.

## Audience
Adults aged 18+ who follow domestic and international football. The primary experience is mobile, with quick scanning of fixture, market, selection, individual price and combined price.

## Jobs to be done
- Understand the day's published accumulator options at a glance.
- Inspect every fixture and market before making any independent decision.
- Compare target odds with the actual combined price.
- Review the latest three daily editions.
- Understand that odds move and outcomes are uncertain.

## Product character
Precise, composed, analytical and football-literate.

## Product constraints
- Football only.
- No promises, guarantees or claims of certain returns.
- Do not expose private data providers, model names, keys or internal processing details in public UI.
- Strong 18+ and responsible-play messaging.
- Results must come from stored, validated data. The browser never invents or recalculates combined odds.
